- **Upper-limit and avoidance habits:** Add “at most” targets, including zero, for habits such as limiting screen time. Require an explicit daily check-in so an empty log does not count as success. Inspired by [Strides’ bad-habit limits](https://www.stridesapp.com/help/).
- **More flexible schedules:** Add every-N-days schedules, monthly targets, and specific dates each month, beyond the current daily/weekday/weekly options. Inspired by [Strides’ recurring schedules](https://www.stridesapp.com/help/).
- **Weekly and monthly quantity goals:** Accumulate minutes, pages, or repetitions across sessions toward a period total, instead of counting only completed days. Extend Streakify’s quantity tracking using ideas from [Strides’ numeric trackers](https://www.stridesapp.com/).
- **Useful reminders:** Add per-habit reminder times, snooze, quiet hours, and check-off actions from notifications; stop nudging once the habit is done. Inspired by [Loop’s actionable reminders](https://github.com/iSoron/uhabits/blob/dev/README.md) and [TickTick’s reminder options](https://ticktick.com/about/features).
- **Habit timers and focus sessions:** Add a stopwatch, countdown, and optional Pomodoro mode that can save elapsed time to a habit after confirmation. Inspired by [Habitify’s built-in timer](https://www.habitify.me/) and [TickTick’s Pomodoro](https://ticktick.com/about/features).
- **Morning and evening routines:** Group habits into an ordered routine with a focused “next step” view and an estimated total duration. Build on the time-of-day organization shown by [Habitify](https://www.habitify.me/).
- **Calendar planning:** Give habits optional time slots and export their schedule to a calendar, with an agenda showing what fits around other plans. Inspired by [Habitify’s calendar integration](https://www.habitify.me/) and [TickTick’s calendar views](https://ticktick.com/about/features).
- **Offline check-ins:** Make the app installable and usable without a connection, queue changes locally, and clearly show pending sync and conflicts when reconnecting. Inspired by [Loop’s offline operation](https://github.com/iSoron/uhabits/blob/dev/README.md); synchronization would be a Streakify addition.
- **Quick-access widgets:** Explore home-screen and lock-screen widgets for today’s habits and quick check-ins on supported platforms. Inspired by [Loop’s widgets](https://github.com/iSoron/uhabits/blob/dev/README.md) and [Streaks’ watch widgets](https://streaksapp.com/).
- **Consistency score alongside streaks:** Show a longer-term habit-strength score so one missed day does not hide months of effort. Inspired by [Loop’s habit score](https://github.com/iSoron/uhabits/blob/dev/README.md).
- **Deeper personal trends:** Extend the current weekly insights with monthly/yearly views, rolling averages, and quantity charts to reveal progress beyond completion counts. Inspired by [Strides’ averages and reports](https://www.stridesapp.com/).
- **Goals with milestones and deadlines:** Link habits to a larger goal, split it into milestones, and show whether progress is on pace for a chosen date. Inspired by [Strides’ Target and Project trackers](https://www.stridesapp.com/).
- **Mood and energy check-ins:** Add a quick optional mood/energy rating and compare it with habit history, presenting patterns without claiming that one caused the other. Inspired by [Habitify’s mood tracking](https://www.habitify.me/).
- **Optional health integrations:** Explore importing steps, workouts, or other chosen health measurements, with clear source labels and duplicate protection. Inspired by [Streaks’ Apple Health integration](https://streaksapp.com/) and [Habitify’s health integrations](https://www.habitify.me/).
- **Personal automations:** Provide scoped API tokens and simple integrations so approved shortcuts or other apps can log progress without sharing the workspace password. Inspired by [Habitify’s API, Zapier, and IFTTT integrations](https://www.habitify.me/).
- **Voice and shortcut logging:** Support quick commands such as “log ten minutes of reading,” with confirmation before saving ambiguous input. Inspired by [Strides’ Siri logging](https://www.stridesapp.com/help/) and [TickTick’s voice input](https://ticktick.com/about/features).
- **Milestone achievements:** Celebrate meaningful totals, completed goals, and consistency milestones with optional badges and subtle animations. Inspired by [Strides’ goal and streak celebrations](https://www.stridesapp.com/).
- **Solo challenges:** Add self-chosen 7-day or 30-day challenges with a clear finish line and a recap, adapting [Habitify’s challenge concept](https://www.habitify.me/) for a private workspace.
- **Optional virtual companion:** Let consistent effort grow a small pet or garden, with an option to hide it. Inspired by [Finch’s self-care pet](https://finchcare.com/).
- **Timed reward sessions:** After redeeming a time-based reward, offer a countdown, pause/resume, and a gentle end alert; keep redemption history separate from whether the session was used. Proposed specifically for Streakify.
- **Dedicated reward savings:** Let coins be reserved toward a chosen larger reward, beyond the current affordability progress bar, with an explicit way to release the reservation. Proposed specifically for Streakify.
- **Milestone reward unlocks:** Optionally require a completed goal or challenge as well as coins before a special reward can be redeemed, and display both requirements clearly. Proposed specifically for Streakify.
